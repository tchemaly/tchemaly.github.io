Augmented Reality ∩ Human Perception ∩ Computer Vision

Trishia El Chemaly

PhD Candidate, Stanford University

I completed my PhD in Bioengineering at Stanford University, co-advised by Dr. Nikolas Blevins and Prof. Brian Hargreaves as part of the IMMERS and Surgical Simulation labs. My PhD research focused on establishing computational and interaction frameworks that enable more accurate and perceptually grounded AR systems for microsurgical guidance. Alongside my research, I served as president of Stanford XR, the university's student organization for extended reality, and co-founded Immerse the Bay, Stanford's first public XR hackathon.

Before Stanford, I was a Research Assistant at the Neural Engineering and Nanobiosensors Group, working on neural interfaces and restoring human vision through electrical stimulation. As an undergraduate, my research focused on the intersection of medical imaging and robotics perception for surgical guidance, with the Robotics and Mechatronics group at the University of Twente in the Netherlands. My goal is to research and build technologies that make a real impact.
